# Test Video Clips

Place your test video files (`.mp4`, `.avi`, `.mov`, `.mkv`) in this folder.
Video files in this folder are ignored by Git.

Recommended test clips:
1. Daytime clip with a person entering the zone (intrusion alert expected)
2. Person walking outside the zone (no alert)
3. Car in a zone that allows cars (authorised, no alert)
4. Car in a person-only zone or unauthorized area (intrusion alert)
5. Person touching the zone briefly below dwell time (pending then cleared, no alert)
6. Night/evening surveillance clip
7. Person leaving and returning within grace period vs after grace period
